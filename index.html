We come to therapy looking for answers.

Sometimes, we leave with acceptance instead.

Just as the love between the fish and the bird—

they may love each other deeply,
but the bird belongs to the sky,
and the fish belongs to the sea.

Neither is wrong for being what they are.

Sometimes, love means accepting that two people
can love each other and still need different worlds.
